How quickly repairs get dealt with

Every maintenance request falls under one of three targets, shown here alongside whatever you currently have open under each. The clock starts when the request is raised — not when it reaches the landlord — so time spent working through it with the assistant counts.

A request's target is worked out from what it says and how it is filed. If a landlord re-files a request, its target is recalculated and the change is recorded on the request's own thread.
